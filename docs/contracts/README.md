# Contracts (drafts)

Drafted on 2026-11-18 from the sourced research in
[docs/research/08-remote-editing-research.md](../research/08-remote-editing-research.md), Part 4.

- **Sign-off.** Each draft needs the business owner's sign-off. A lawyer's review is optional (decision L37).
- **Company details.** They are placeholders in [brackets] until the company is registered (decision 10).

| Document | Language | For | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| [terms-of-service.md](terms-of-service.md) | English | Every remote-editing client: scope, limits, delivery times, revisions, payment, the founding offer, cancellation, rights in the edits, AI use, liability | Part of every order; published at `/en/terms` |
| [data-processing-agreement.md](data-processing-agreement.md) | English | Clients whose footage shows people. It covers GDPR Art. 28, with the Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 4) for US clients outside the Data Privacy Framework and for Australian clients | Sent with the order confirmation |
| [rights-assignment-bg.md](rights-assignment-bg.md) | Bulgarian | The editor (the business owner as an individual) and any freelancer, toward the company. Only a person can be an author, so the company needs the rights before it can grant them to clients | Signed once, before the first order |

The Bulgarian filming business's own client contract is a separate document, still to be drafted. It follows
[docs/research/07-business-research.md](../research/07-business-research.md), Part 5 §4.3.
