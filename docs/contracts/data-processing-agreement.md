## Data Processing Agreement

This agreement is between:
- **the client**, who orders video editing services and decides why and how the footage is used (the
  **controller**);
- **Hera Editing** ([legal name], [ЕИК], [address], Bulgaria; contact@hera-editing.com), which edits the footage on
  the client's behalf (the **processor**).

It applies whenever the client's footage or materials contain personal data, and forms part of the Terms of Service.

### 1. What is processed, and why

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Editing the client's footage into the deliverables ordered, and delivering them |
| **Personal data** | The images and voices of people who appear in the footage; names and other details shown in titles or captions; the contact details of the client's team |
| **Special categories** | Only if the client's footage contains them, for example health information in a clinic's video. The client tells us in the brief |
| **People concerned** | The people who appear in the footage; the client's staff and contacts |
| **Duration** | For the order, then until deletion under §8 |
| **Operations** | Receiving, storing, viewing, editing, transcribing, captioning, rendering, sharing review links, delivering and deleting |

### 2. Instructions

The processor processes the personal data only on the client's documented instructions: the order, the brief and
written messages. The processor tells the client if, in its view, an instruction breaks data-protection law.

### 3. Confidentiality

Everyone who works on the footage for the processor is bound by confidentiality.

### 4. Security (Art. 32)

The processor protects the data with appropriate measures:
- personal accounts with two-factor authentication on every storage, review and editing service;
- access limited to the people working on the order;
- encryption in transit for uploads, downloads and review links;
- review links that are private and expire;
- no copies on removable media;
- deletion on schedule (§8).

### 5. Sub-processors

**General authorisation.** The client authorises the processor to use the sub-processors in Annex 2. The processor
tells the client in writing at least 14 days before adding or replacing one, and the client may object on reasonable
grounds. The processor binds each sub-processor to data-protection obligations equivalent to these.

**Responsibility.** The processor remains responsible for its sub-processors.

### 6. Helping the client

The processor helps the client, as far as it reasonably can:
- answer requests from the people in the footage, such as a request to remove someone or blur a face;
- meet its security, breach-notification and impact-assessment duties (Arts. 32–36).

### 7. Personal data breaches

The processor notifies the client without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting
the client's data. The notice gives:
- what happened;
- the data and people affected;
- the likely consequences;
- the measures taken.

### 8. Deletion and return

The processor deletes the client's raw footage and project files 30 days after final delivery, and the final files 30
days after delivery, unless the client asks in writing for earlier deletion or longer storage. On request, it confirms
the deletion in writing.

### 9. Information and audits

The processor makes available the information needed to show compliance with this agreement. It answers reasonable
written audit questions within 14 days.

### 10. Transfers outside the EEA

**When the clauses apply.** The processor works in the EU, in Bulgaria. Returning or delivering data to a client in a
country without an EU adequacy decision is a transfer under Art. 44. That includes US clients not certified under the
EU–US Data Privacy Framework, and clients in Australia. For those transfers, the parties agree to the **Standard
Contractual Clauses, Module 4 (processor to controller)**, adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.

**How they are incorporated.** They are incorporated by reference, with Annex 1 below as their Annex I.
- Clause 17, governing law: the law of Bulgaria.
- Clause 18, courts: the courts of Bulgaria.

**Clients in an adequate country.** For clients in the UK, in Canada (commercial organisations), or in the US and
certified under the Data Privacy Framework, the relevant adequacy decision applies instead.

### 11. Law

This agreement is governed by Bulgarian law and the GDPR. If it conflicts with the Terms of Service on data
protection, this agreement prevails.
