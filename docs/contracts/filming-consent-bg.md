# Съгласие за заснемане и публикуване (per person, every shoot)

- **Status:** draft 0.1, 2026-11-18. It needs the business owner's sign-off. A lawyer's review is optional (decision
  L37).
- **Why it is needed.** A form like this is required for anyone featured:
  - anyone recognisable and central to the shot;
  - anyone giving a testimonial;
  - anyone mid-treatment.

  Consent must be written and specific. The person must be able to withdraw it. Children need their parents' consent,
  plus their own from age 14.
- **Basis:** `docs/research/07-business-research.md` Part 4, "Filming customers, staff and children":
  - GDPR Arts. 6, 7 and 9;
  - EDPB 05/2020;
  - the КЗЛД opinion of 12 Feb 2026;
  - ЗЗДет art. 11а;
  - ЗЗЛД art. 25в.
- **How to use it:**
  - One form per person per shoot. Keep it in the consent register.
  - People in the background get a notice on site and an easy way out of the shot instead.
  - Never film people "in a vulnerable state".
