**What the market does.** 11 sources publish something about commitment:

- **3 months as a hard minimum (2):** SocialDany (on its monthly content plan only) and Silvia Zhivova Academy.
- **3 months as the standard option (2):** Netpeak (3 or 6 months) and EVOL ("стандартни тримесечни договори", otherwise flexible).
- **6 months (2):** Florov links his price to a 6-month contract. Adline requires 6 months, but only for monthly consulting.
- **No minimum (1):** Wonder states it explicitly, but still keeps a one-month notice period.
- **Priced per month, minimum not mentioned (2):** Next Level Digital and Moon.

The 3LS blog tells buyers that a one-month notice is standard. Three agencies (e-motion, AdExpert, Wonder) describe month 1 as set-up, with results from month 2–3.

Nothing published: Bridges, SEOMAX, MC Productions, In Motion, DigitaL+.

**Recommended wording for Hera Editing**

> Обикновено препоръчваме първоначален период от 3 месеца.
>
> Така има време за настройка, първите снимки и първите изводи от данните.
>
> Точния срок уточняваме в индивидуалната Ви оферта.
>
> *(По избор)* След това обикновено продължаваме месец за месец, с едномесечно предизвестие.

*Rationale:* 3 months is the most common published commitment: a hard minimum at SocialDany and Zhivova, and the standard option at Netpeak and EVOL. It also matches the "results from month 2–3" message from e-motion, AdExpert and Wonder. Saying "препоръчваме" rather than "минимум" keeps Hera softer than the 6-month outliers. The optional one-month notice is what SocialDany (30 days), Wonder and 3LS publish.
