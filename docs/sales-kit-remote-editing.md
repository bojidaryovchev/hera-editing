# Remote editing: the sales kit (ready to use)

- **What it is.** Texts and briefs for the remote-editing line ([strategy-remote-editing.md](strategy-remote-editing.md)),
  ready to copy. Every English text is written to be sent as is. Change the parts in [brackets].
- **Prices.** They are the plan's proposals (strategy §5), until the business owner changes them. Launch prices are 30%
  off the list price. Fiverr prices after the first 5 reviews are the list price ÷ 0.8, because Fiverr keeps 20%.
- **What launch prices net.** After Fiverr's 20%, a launch price nets about 56% of the list price, for example $213 for
  the wedding highlight. Keep launch prices only until the first 5 reviews.
- **Contents:**
  1. Fiverr gigs
  2. YTJobs application
  3. Outreach emails
  4. Client message templates
  5. The brief form
  6. Sample briefs

## 1. Fiverr gigs

Three gigs, one per test niche. A Fiverr gig has three tiers (Basic, Standard and Premium), a description, an FAQ, the
questions the buyer answers when ordering, and tags.

Before publishing:
- check the category names in Fiverr's seller dashboard;
- add 3 samples to each gig's gallery, labelled "Demo edit" where they are not client work.

### Gig 1: wedding films for filmmakers

**Title:** I will edit your wedding film from your footage, for filmmakers and studios

| Tier | Deliverable | Launch price | After 5 reviews | Delivery | Revisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | Teaser, 60–90 s, in 16:9 and 9:16 | $112 | $200 | 4 days | 2 |
| Standard | Highlight film, 4–6 min, from your selected clips (up to 90 min) plus the vows and speech audio | $266 | $475 | 7 days | 2 |
| Premium | Trio: highlight from selects, a documentary full film (up to 60 min) and a teaser | $602 | $1,075 | 10 days | 2 |

**Description:**

```
Wedding filmmakers and studios: send me your footage, get back a finished film in your style.

I edit wedding highlights, full documentary films and teasers for filmmakers who want to shoot more and edit less.
You keep your style: share your LUT, a past film you love and your music licence, and I match them.

What you get:
- A story built around the vows and speeches, cut to your licensed music
- Colour matched to your look, clean audio mix, titles
- 16:9 masters, plus 9:16 versions of teasers for social media
- Two revision rounds, with time-coded comments on a review link

How it works: upload your footage (Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer), fill in the short brief, and I start when
your slot opens. Everything is in writing, so there are no calls to schedule.

I am based in Europe. I edit while you sleep, and every package states its delivery time in days.

Unculled footage, longer films or extra versions? Send me a message for a custom quote.
```

**FAQ:**

```
Q: Do you cut from all my footage or from selects?
A: The Standard highlight is cut from your selected clips (up to 90 minutes) plus the vows and speech audio. If you
   want me to cull the full footage, I quote it separately.

Q: Who provides the music?
A: You do: please send the track and its licence. I do not supply or resell music licences.

Q: Can you match my style?
A: Yes. Send a past film, your LUT or grade notes, and any titles or fonts you use.

Q: How do revisions work?
A: Each round is one list of time-coded notes on the review link. Two rounds are included.

Q: Do you work with couples directly?
A: My wedding service is for filmmakers and studios.
```

**Questions for the buyer when ordering:**
- your footage link;
- the music track and licence;
- a reference film or your LUT;
- the couple's names for the titles;
- the key moments to include;
- your deadline.

**Tags:** wedding video editing, wedding film editor, wedding highlight, video editing, filmmaker

story-driven and documentary videos from a script and voice-over, and I can match that style.

Samples close to your channel: [link 1], [link 2].

How I work: everything in writing (brief, review links with time-coded comments, delivery). I am in Europe, so I
edit while you sleep. A [length]-minute video like yours takes me [N] business days.

Happy to do a short test edit if that is part of your process.

[Name]
[Portfolio link]
```

Apply to 5–10 documentary, scripted or faceless posts a week. Write the first sentence yourself every time. Posts that
say "no AI proposals" reject templates that read as generic.

## 3. Outreach emails

**Rules for every email** (strategy §9):
- **One at a time,** to a business address the company publishes.
- **Never sent through the site's mail service.**
- **Identification:** the sender's name and company. US law (CAN-SPAM) also asks for a postal address.
- **An opt-out line,** and every "no" honoured.
- **UK:** only to companies and LLPs. Sole traders and partnerships need consent first.

Hi [name], thanks for reaching out. Yes, I can help with [project]. To give you an exact price and date, please fill
in this short brief: [brief link]. Your next free slot starts on [date].

QUESTIONS ABOUT THE BRIEF
Thanks for the brief. Two questions before I start: 1) [question] 2) [question]. Once I have your answers, I will
start on [date] and deliver by [date].

DELIVERY
Your first cut is ready: [review link]. Please add time-coded comments directly on the video and press "done" when you
have finished. All notes in one round, please; your package includes [N] rounds.

REVISION
The revised version is here: [review link]. I made these changes: [short list]. Let me know if anything else needs
adjusting; this was round [N] of [N].

QUOTE
Here is the quote for [project]: [deliverables], from up to [footage limit], [N] revision rounds, delivered in [N]
business days from the start date. Price: $[amount]. 50% upfront, the rest before the final files. Valid for 14 days.

FEEDBACK
Thanks for working with me on [project]. If you have a minute, an honest review would help me a lot. Whatever you
think of the work, I'd like to hear it.
```

Never offer anything in exchange for a review (strategy §5).

## 5. The brief form (fields)

**For every order:**
- name and company;
- email;
- country;
- the package or deliverable;
- a link to the footage;
- the deadline;
- reference videos, with timestamps;
- who supplies the music, with the licence;
- whether AI-generated visuals are allowed;
- anything that must or must not appear;
- confirmation that the client is a business, with the business name or VAT number. This proof is needed for VAT,
  strategy §9.

**Added per niche:**
- **Weddings:** the couple's names, the key moments, the vows and speech files, a LUT or grade notes, a past film for
  the style.
- **YouTube:** the script, the voice-over file, the channel link, the stock library.
- **Real estate:** the address or listing name, the room order or floor plan, branding files, branded or unbranded
  versions.

## 6. Sample briefs (what to make first)

**Rules for samples:**
- **3 per niche.** Each is labelled "Demo edit" unless it is real client work.
- **Footage:** his own, a client's with written permission, or licensed. [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/)
  allows free use and modification without attribution. People must not appear in a bad light, and no endorsement may
  be implied. Check every clip's licence on its page.
- **Music:** royalty-free with a licence that covers the use, recorded in the licence log.

| Niche | Sample | Length | Footage | What it must show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Real estate | 1. Apartment or house listing | 60–90 s + 30 s reel | **Film a property in Bulgaria himself**, with the owner's written permission. This is the fastest real sample, since he is a filmmaker | A smooth walk-through order, stabilisation, colour, music sync, branded and unbranded versions |
| Real estate | 2. Luxury listing | 60–90 s | Licensed house-tour clips (Pexels) | Premium pacing, titles, drone shots if available |
| Real estate | 3. Vertical-first reel | 20–30 s | Same footage as 1 | Hooks in the first 2 seconds, captions |
| YouTube | 1. Documentary short | 4–6 min | A short script on a public topic, licensed stock B-roll, and a voice-over. An AI voice is acceptable for a demo, but say so in the description | Story structure, B-roll matching, maps or motion text, sound design |
| YouTube | 2. Faceless explainer | 3–5 min | Licensed stock, text-led, no voice-over | Text animation, pacing, music |
| YouTube | 3. Shorts from sample 1 | 3 × 30–45 s | Sample 1 | Re-cut for 9:16, hooks, captions |
| Weddings | 1. Highlight | 3–4 min | **A Bulgarian wedding videographer's footage**, with the videographer's and the couple's written permission (a natural partner for the core business too); or licensed wedding clips | Vows-led story, music cut, grade |
| Weddings | 2. Teaser | 60 s, 16:9 + 9:16 | Same footage | Rhythm, the best moments, the vertical version |
| Weddings | 3. Full-film excerpt | 3–5 min of a documentary edit | Same footage | Clean multicam, ceremony audio, titles |

**Where samples live.** Unlisted YouTube or Vimeo links for the gigs and emails, then the portfolio page of the English
section.
