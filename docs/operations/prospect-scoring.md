# Prospect scoring (the outreach list)

- **What it is.** The rubric of spec/research/04: six criteria, each scored 0–2. Prospects scoring 9 or more are
  contacted first.
- **What it is not.** The spec calls it "an outreach priority heuristic, not a statistically calibrated conversion
  predictor."
- **How to use it.** Use the columns below for the outreach list, for the Bulgarian line and for remote editing.

| Criterion | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Locality** (Bulgaria) / **time zone and language** (abroad) | within the filming radius / US or UK, briefs in writing | travel feasible for a fee / other English-speaking | not viable |
| **Visual opportunity** | something to film daily or weekly | moderate | little |
| **Repeatable topics** | many | occasional | scarce |
| **Budget signal** (not proof of ability to pay) | already spends on marketing; a healthy business | unsure | clearly too small |
| **Access** | a clear contact person and permission to film | unclear | impossible |
| **Strategic fit** | wants a recurring, outsourced workflow | a one-off | needs something else |
