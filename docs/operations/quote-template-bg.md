- **What it is.** The written quote sent after the first call. It follows spec/research/03 §9, which says a quote must
  show:
  - the price in EUR and the VAT status;
  - travel and the number of shoots;
  - original videos against platform versions, and their formats;
  - the number of channels;
  - moderation;
  - the revision limits.
