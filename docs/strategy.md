## 2. The offer

### 2.1 What Hera sells

The specification ([spec/product/01](../spec/product/01-service-model-and-economics.md)) describes it as a monthly team
that **comes to the business, films original material, edits it into a planned calendar, publishes after approval and
reports**.

| Plan | Original short videos a month | Shoots | Platforms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Старт | 4 | 1 | up to 2 |
| Растеж | 8 | 1–2 | up to 3 |
| Индивидуално | individually scoped, often 12 or more | custom | custom |

- Paid ads are excluded.
- **Comment monitoring** is optional and bounded.
- **Public replies and message handling** are included only when the offer says so.
- Prices are by individual offer.

### 2.2 Where it sits in the market

Part 2 §1.2 sorts the published offers into tiers.

| Tier | What the client gets | Monthly price | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Posting only | designed static posts | €30–510, usually €150–350 | Wonder, Moon, Ideo |
| Design and posting | Reels edited from the client's own footage; no filming | €150–820 | Templa Старт €549, Bo Arts, Zhivova |
| Video production | filming included; little or no publishing | €200–900 | SocialDany €480, 6th Frame €500–900, Templa Само Reels €790, Next Level Silver €899.99 (no platform management) |
| **Full production and management (Hera's tier)** | filming, editing, publishing, reporting | **€800–1,800** | Templa Растеж €1,049 ✓, Next Level Gold and Platinum €999.99–1,299.99 ✓, STONER from €900, e-motion from €800; Public Chapter publishes no price (Part 3) |
| AI price floor | AI videos made from the client's photos; no filming | €49–79 | Raboti |

**What makes Hera different:**
- Filming every month is the core of the service. Most offers edit the client's own footage, or film without publishing.
- Honest reporting with no promised numbers. Some rivals promise "1000 гарантирани нови фенове" or guaranteed reach.
- A website that itself looks like video work.

Approval before publishing is expected in this market, so it reassures but does not set Hera apart. Hera has it twice:
the plan and scripts before filming, and the finished video before publishing.

**What Hera lacks today:**
- Any proof: no clients, case studies, team names, testimonials or reviews.
- Replies to comments and messages as a standard part of the plan.
- A published price.

**The line to sell with:** "Your own small video team every month, for less than one employee." A full-time content
hire costs €1,400–2,100 a month all-in (Part 2 §3.5).

### 2.3 Three changes worth considering

1. **Comment replies as a paid add-on**: weekday monitoring and replies to comments and messages, within agreed
   limits.
   - The nearest rival at 8 videos includes them (Templa Растеж, Monday to Friday).
   - Published add-on prices: Flashgate €8–16 a day; Wonder "by volume".
   - Suggested price: about €150–300 a month, depending on volume. This is an owner decision.
2. **A free first consultation with concrete ideas.** Nine of 16 competitors offer a free consultation or audit.
   - Hera's main call to action is „Поискайте оферта“, the spec's own wording, so changing it is a v1.1 change.
   - See section 9.
3. **A client phone kit inside the service.** A one-page shot list, a framing guide and caption templates.
   - With them the client films the moments a monthly shoot cannot catch: specials, new stock, events. Hera edits a few
     of those clips each month (Part 4, "What one monthly visit can realistically yield").
   - Platforms reward consistency: three posts a week need about 13 videos a month, and one visit yields 6–15,
     depending on the sector.
   - The number of client clips edited each month belongs in the offer.

**Add-ons for later,** once the core works:
- extra shoot days;
- photos;
- managing the client's Google Business Profile, as their authorised representative under Google's rules (Part 5
  §2.4);
- paid ads, as a separate future quote, as the spec already says.

Product photography is the largest commercial search next to the service: „продуктова фотография“, 140 a month ✓.

## 3. Who buys

### 3.1 The affordability line

Part 1 §2 assumes a business spends 2–5% of its turnover on marketing.

- **Turnover needed.** For a fee of €8,000–15,000 a year, a client needs a turnover of about €160,000–750,000.
- **Micro firms.** In the target sectors, firms with 0–9 people average €20,000–70,000 a year, so even the €8,000 entry
  fee would be 12–39% of their turnover.
- **Firms with 10 or more people** clear the bar.

The target is therefore firms with about 10 or more people, plus smaller firms with unusually high turnover: a busy
clinic, a car dealer, a developer.

Firms with 10 or more persons employed, 2024 (Part 1 §2.3):

**How many clients Hera needs (estimate):**
- **Hours per client.** The spec's worked example puts one client's month at about 35 hours. It is illustrative and
  does not say how many videos it covers.
- **Hours per person.** A full-time person has about 170 working hours a month, but not all of them are billable:
  sales, admin and Hera's own accounts take time.
- **Capacity.** So one person can serve about 3–4 such clients, and two people about 6–8. That is under 0.1% of the
  pool.
- **The real constraint.** The market is not the limit. The work is reaching the right few hundred firms within
  filming distance and convincing them (8.2).

### 3.4 Where

- **Sofia** has 34.4% of the firms with 10+ people and 48% of all turnover.
- **The four biggest districts** (Sofia, Plovdiv, Varna and Burgas) together have 58% of these firms.
- **Core pool by district (estimate):** Sofia about 3,700, Plovdiv about 1,100, Varna about 900, Burgas about 900.
- **Hotels** are concentrated on the coast: Burgas district has 35.8% of tourist nights and Varna 17.2%.

**The base city is the first owner decision.** It fixes the filming radius, the outreach list, the Google Business
Profile service area and the FAQ's question 3.

### 3.5 Who is not a fit

- **Micro cafés, solo salons and personal trainers.** They cannot sustain the fee. AI tools at €49–79 and freelancers
  serve them.
- **Chains with central marketing.**
- **Anyone who wants guaranteed sales or followers, or only wants ads.**
- **Businesses that want content the rules forbid:**
  - clinics that want price promotions, before-and-after pictures of injectables, or boasting claims;
  - bars that want spirits promotions;
  - hookah or vape lounges, since online tobacco and vape advertising is banned.

  Hera shares the liability for misleading adverts it prepares (section 7).
- **Businesses far from the base with no travel budget.**

## 4. What they want, and what they object to

### 4.1 The job they hire Hera for

The spec's buyer jobs ([spec/research/02](../spec/research/02-customer-segments-and-seo.md)):
- "I want our brand to look credible and active without doing production personally."
- "I need someone to decide what to post, arrive with equipment, make it, post it and report back."
- "We have activity worth showing but nobody organizes shoots."
- "I need a reliable outsourced team, not a different freelancer for every step."

The prices back the last one: assembling 8 videos a month from freelancers costs about €560–1,930, and the owner still
has to coordinate them (Part 2 §2).

### 4.2 Objections and answers

These objections come from competitors' FAQs and the spec's discovery guide. Confirm them in the first calls.

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "It costs as much as an employee." | One full-time content person costs €1,400–2,100 a month all-in, and rarely plans, films and edits equally well. Hera's 8-video plan is about €1,000–1,100. A café owner compares the fee with a part-timer (€540–610) or a hospitality salary (€896–930 gross ✓), so start them on 4 videos. |
| "Will it bring customers?" | No guaranteed numbers (the spec's claims rules). Every month the report shows what was measured. The spec's 90-day cadence brings initial signals after the first posts in weeks 3–4 and the first monthly review in month 2. A fair judgement takes 3 months, the recommended initial period. |
| "I don't have time." | One planning meeting, the shoot itself, and approving the scripts and finished videos within the agreed window, through one contact person. |
| "Who owns the account and the videos?" | The client owns the profiles, and Hera works through delegated roles, never passwords. The footage needs a contract clause, for two reasons (Part 5 §4.4). Without a clause, the author keeps the copyright, and the client may use the work only for the purpose it was commissioned for (ЗАПСП art. 42). And a licence that leaves out exclusivity, term or territory defaults to non-exclusive, 3 years and Bulgaria only (art. 36). So the contract must spell all of it out. |
| "Can I cancel?" | Today the site says Hera usually recommends a first period of 3 months, with the exact term in the offer. The market norm, recommended for the sign-off, is 3 months, then monthly with 1 month's notice (Part 5 §4.6). |
| "Why not just run ads?" | Ads buy reach for content; they do not make it. Only 25.6% of firms pay for online ads. Hera's videos can be used in ads too, if the contract licenses that use and the music and the people filmed allow it. |
| "We don't want to be on camera." | Already answered on the site (question 6): the process, the product and the place can carry a video. |
| "Can we use popular songs?" | No business account may. Meta's music guidelines prohibit commercial use without licences ✓. TikTok's business terms require its commercial library or pre-cleared music ✓. Hera uses each platform's commercial library or a licensed library, and asks for the venue's music to be switched off while filming (section 7.3). |

### 5.2 Recommendation (to validate in the first calls)

- **4 videos: about €600.** This is the entry plan and the natural start for hospitality, where the comparison is a
  part-time employee.
- **8 videos: about €1,000–1,050 for the first clients.**
  - At €1,100 Hera stands 5–10% above both nearest rivals (assuming Next Level's price excludes VAT), and unlike
    Templa it does not answer comments.
  - To charge €1,100, add visible value: comment replies, or a second shoot each month.
- **12 or more videos: from €1,300.**
- **Comment replies as an add-on,** about €150–300 a month (section 2.3).
- **Measure real hours on the first three clients,** then set the price list.
  - The spec's illustrative month: 35 hours at €18 an hour loaded, plus €90 of expenses, costs €720.
  - At €1,000 that leaves a gross margin of about 28%; at €1,050, about 31%.
  - The example uses 35%, but the spec says to set the margin with the actual team.

### 5.3 VAT and invoices (Part 5 §4)

- **Registration.** It becomes mandatory once taxable turnover passes **€51,130 in a calendar year** ✓, and the
  application is due within 7 days.
  - That is about 5 clients at €1,000, or 4 at €1,100 or more.
  - Plan for it within the first year.
- **The trap: art. 97а.** Buying services from abroad can force an earlier, special registration. Examples: Meta or
  Google ads, a music or stock library, software. Ask the accountant before the first such purchase.
- **How to quote.** State every price "без ДДС" from the first offer, with a clause that VAT is added once Hera
  registers, so prices do not have to change later. The accountant should confirm the wording.
- **Who pays VAT as a real cost.**
  - For VAT-registered clients, Hera's VAT is neutral. Most firms with 10+ people must be registered, since their
    turnover is far above the threshold (an inference).
  - **Two exceptions.** Clinics: medical services are VAT-exempt (ЗДДС art. 39 ✓), so they usually cannot recover it.
    And any client that is not registered. For both, Hera's VAT will be a real 20% on top.
  - The accountant should confirm.
- **Invoices.** They can be in euro only (no dual display for B2B), issued within 5 days, with 10-digit numbering.

### 5.4 When to publish prices

- Six of the 16 competitor sites assessed publish prices, and so do both nearest rivals (Templa and Next Level).
- Buyers ask about price early: it is the site's own question 4 and in Public Chapter's FAQ, and the price searches
  show small but real demand.
- **Recommendation:**
  - keep "по индивидуална оферта" until the first 3 pilot clients have shown the real hours;
  - then publish "from" prices on `/paketi`;
  - until then, give the range in the first call.

This follows the spec's own rule: revisit public prices after 3 paid pilots.

### 6.2 What the platforms reward

Part 4, "Every platform pays for watch-through and originality":
- **Watch time and completion** on all four video platforms; Meta and TikTok also count shares. None sets a posting

- **Originality as a gate, not a bonus:**
  - Instagram recommends only the original of identical copies and excludes visible watermarks.
  - Facebook demotes duplicates and recycled content.
  - TikTok keeps "unoriginal or reused material" out of its For You feed.
  - YouTube's reuse rules concern monetisation only.
  - Footage the agency films for the client's own account is original under every one of these definitions.
- **What the platforms say about length and openings:**
  - Instagram calls 15–60 seconds "the sweet spot".
  - Facebook says reels of 10–30 seconds "drive reach".
  - TikTok: "the first 3–6 seconds are crucial".
  - YouTube Shorts: uploads up to 3 minutes.
  - Google Business Profile: video uploads up to 30 seconds, with no added text.
- **On TikTok, follower count is not a direct ranking factor,** so a small local account can reach beyond its
  followers.

### 6.3 How Hera should publish

1. **One clean master.** Edit one 9:16 master with no platform watermark. Keep text inside a conservative safe zone:
   about 14% free at the top, 35% at the bottom and 6% at each side (Meta's ads guide).
2. **Native uploads.** Upload it from the client's own account on each platform.
   - Never download from one app to repost on another.
   - Never post the same file to several Facebook Pages.
3. **Captions per platform.** Write each one separately, keywords first, with at most five hashtags on Meta.
4. **A Google cut.** Make a version of 30 seconds or less, with no burned-in text, for the Business Profile.
5. **Music and disclosure.** Use only commercial music (section 7.3). Use TikTok's disclosure setting for promotions;
   its rules require it.
6. **Reports.** Lead with what the client can count: calls, bookings, messages, listing-code enquiries, reviews. Reach
   comes after. YouTube now counts a Shorts view from the first frame, so report its "engaged views" instead.

### 7.2 Rules that limit content

**Hera shares the liability.**
- The agency that prepares a misleading advert is liable alongside the advertiser (ЗЗК art. 32(2)).
- The consumer regulator can order the agency itself to publish a correction (ЗЗП art. 68л(3)).
- So a pre-publication check for each regulated sector is part of the product, not overhead (Part 4, "Clinics answer to
  four rulebooks").

Rules marked ✓ were re-checked on lex.bg; the evidence file's preface lists them.

| Segment | What the rules say | House rule for Hera |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurants and bars | Direct advertising of spirits (15% alcohol or more) is banned in any medium ✓ (ЗЗ art. 55(1)). Wine and beer may be shown, but not aimed at minors or linked to driving or sport. Indoor smoking is banned, and so is online tobacco and vape advertising. A "−50%" or happy-hour post must show the prior price and the period | food, people and the room come first; wine or beer with food, adults only; never shots, spirit brands, drinking games or smoking on camera |
| Clinics | Medical specialists and medical establishments "may not use commercial advertising" ✓ (ЗЗ art. 190(2)), a term the law does not define. The dentists' code allows profiles, but bans prices, promotions and laudatory or comparative content online, and makes the dentist answer for whoever runs the profile. Prescription medicines such as botulinum toxin may not be advertised online. TikTok bars the marketing of licensed medical services by default | education, not advertising: the team, the technology, patients' questions answered. No prices, discounts, product names, or before-and-after of needle or surgical procedures. The doctor approves every post. A lawyer reviews the checklist before the first clinic |
| Real estate | Where an energy certificate exists, its energy figure (kWh/m²) must appear in every advert ✓ (ЗЕЕ art. 34(4)). A post misleads if its overall impression deceives, even when the facts are accurate: edited views, wide-angle tricks | the energy figure in every caption where a certificate exists; no edit that changes what a buyer would see |
| Hotels | Only certified places may use "СПА", "уелнес", "балнеолечебен (медикъл СПА)" or "таласотерапия". The category must not mislead, prices must be in euro, and "eco" claims need proof. No offer posts on the Google profile | check the certificate before using those words |
| Gyms and salons | Food supplements may not claim a rate of weight loss, and no product may claim to cure. A salon may not imply medical procedures. The data regulator calls filming during cosmetic procedures, especially undressed, impermissible | transformation videos only with written consent; never needle procedures |
| Every sector | A price cut needs the prior price and the period. "Tag three friends" giveaways breach Facebook's terms, and purchase-linked draws may count as gambling. Paid creators must be disclosed (#реклама). Drones must be registered and insured, and must not fly over crowds | one checklist per post type |

### 7.3 People and music

- **Written consent from anyone featured.** That means anyone recognisable and central to the shot, giving a
  testimonial, or mid-treatment.
  - **Background people:** a notice on site and an easy way out of the shot.
  - **Staff:** separate written consent, with a genuine option not to appear.
  - **Children:** the parents' consent, plus the child's own from age 14.
  - **Never film people "in a vulnerable state".** The data regulator names restaurants and gyms among the settings.
  - **If someone withdraws consent,** take the post down or edit them out.
- **No trending music.** Business accounts may not use popular tracks: Meta's music guidelines ✓, TikTok's business
  terms ✓.
  - Use each platform's commercial library, which works only on that platform, or a licensed library with an agency
    plan.
  - Keep a licence record for every track.
  - Check a library's agency terms before subscribing. Part 4 cites Epidemic Sound's business licence (13 April 2026):
    it caps agencies at five end clients a month and USD 5m turnover. The page could not be re-opened to confirm this.
  - A subscription from a foreign supplier can trigger VAT registration under art. 97а (section 5.3).
  - Ask for the venue's music to be switched off while filming.

### 8.3 The outreach rules (Part 5 §1; for the lawyer to confirm)

- **Email to businesses without prior consent is lawful** (ЗЕТ art. 6) if all of these hold:

  - the sender is identified;
  - an opt-out address is given;
  - every refusal is honoured.
- **Private individuals need prior consent** for email, SMS, messages and calls. Never approach them.
- **A named employee's address is personal data.** Before the first email:
